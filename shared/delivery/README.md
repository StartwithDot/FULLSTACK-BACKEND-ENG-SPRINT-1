# Delivery evidence

These files are authored by students, not prefilled proof of success.

- Week 1: brief.md and context.md.
- Each week: assign bounded tasks and record real owners, PRs and evidence in rotations/week-NN.md.
- Week 5: first-flow.md.
- Week 9: ownership.md.
- Week 11: browser-flow.md.
- Week 12: test-matrix.md.
- Week 14: incident.md and clean-start.md.
- Week 15: runbook.md, final-architecture.md, demo.md and the witnessed contribution record.

The [weekly plan](../../docs/08-shared-build-plan.md) is authoritative when its paths are more specific. Record real commands, PRs/commits, observations and limitations. Do not claim milestones from the starter tests alone.

A non-author performs the final handover. Assigned owners write or promote the documentation; others review or reproduce it during the same weekly shared slot. A maintainer reassigns unavailable owners/reviewers. These records track actual work, not a fixed pair schedule or private reasons for absence.
